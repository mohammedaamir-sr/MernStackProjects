Ask Me Anything

I love answering questions about design, engineering, and entrepreneurship. Feel free to reach out!

How do you approach a new design project?

I always start with understanding the problem and the user. I conduct thorough research, create user personas, and map out user journeys. Then, I move to ideation, wireframing, and prototyping before jumping into high-fidelity design.

What's the best way to learn web development?

Learn by building projects! Start with HTML/CSS basics, then move to JavaScript. Build real projects, solve actual problems, and don't just follow tutorials. Read code from others, ask questions, and contribute to open source.

How do you manage ADHD as a designer and engineer?

ADHD can actually be a superpower in creative fields. I've learned to channel hyperfocus into deep work sessions, use tools for organization, take breaks when needed, and build a supportive team that understands my working style.

What advice would you give to aspiring entrepreneurs?

Focus on solving real problems, not just ideas. Build an MVP quickly, get feedback from users, and iterate. Surround yourself with great people, stay adaptable, and remember that consistency matters more than perfection.

How do you stay updated with design and tech trends?

I follow design and tech blogs, participate in online communities, attend conferences, and most importantly, I experiment with new tools and technologies in my own projects. Learning by doing is the best approach.
